How I read the role

The Orléans Campus needs its people, from lab researchers to line operators, to feel part of the House and work as one site. Today the corporate message is written for Paris and the boutiques, and each activity speaks for itself.
  • "Structure and energize"The Campus is building its communication function now: master plan, budget, team.
  • "Adapt corporate communications"Messages designed for Paris need a version that makes sense in a lab or on a production line.
  • "Sense of belonging" and "collective pride"People on an industrial site can feel far from the image of the brand they work for.
  • "Connections between activities"R&D, production and supply chain rarely see each other's work.
  • "Realities of the various roles"Many people on site have no desk and no work email, so the usual channels miss them.

How I would start

My first six months on the Campus

A first draft, written from the outside. The first weeks would be spent checking it against what people on site actually tell me.

Weeks 1 to 4

Listen

  • Meet each department head
  • Spend time on the lines and in the labs
  • Map every channel that exists today
  • Find out who has no screen access

Weeks 5 to 8

Structure

  • Draft the Campus communication master plan

  • Simple indicators to track reach

Weeks 9 to 12

Launch

  • First video portraits of Campus employees
  • Screens and posters in break areas

Months 4 to 6

Measure and adjust

  • Short pulse survey on belonging
  • Review what reached the shop floor

Ideas I would bring

A few concrete starting points

Faces of the Campus

A short monthly video portrait of one person, alternating between a researcher, an operator, a logistician and a support role. Shown on site screens and on the intranet, so each activity sees the others at work.

From the lab to the boutique

Follow one product from formula to shelf, filmed in each department that touches it. It shows everyone where their work ends up, which is the most direct link to the House I can think of.

Channels for people without a desk

Posters, screens in break rooms and short videos on mobile, designed for a two-minute break. I already design in-store posters and vertical videos for exactly this kind of short attention.

A toolkit for departments

Templates, a one-page writing guide and a request form, so departments can share news in the Campus style and come to me for the bigger projects.
